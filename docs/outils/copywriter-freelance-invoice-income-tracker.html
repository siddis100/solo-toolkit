Frequently asked questions

How much tax does a copywriter pay on $43 200 of revenue?

At 30 % + 0 % CFP, $43 200 of revenue leaves roughly $12 960.0 in contributions, so about $30 239.999999999996 before income tax.

Is this calculation final?

No. Rates depend on your activity, on first-year relief and on year-end adjustments. Use it as a planning estimate, not as a tax return.

Do I need an account to use the calculator?

No. Everything runs in your browser, offline, with no signup and no cookie.

Can I keep a full-year record?

Yes. The spreadsheet version keeps 12 months of income and expenses, so you can see profit, margin and tax reserve in one place.
