Frequently asked questions

Why only three priorities?

Working memory holds about three live items. Three is enough to matter and small enough to start, even on a low-energy day.

Does the planner need an account?

No. Tasks and energy level are saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only.

What does the 25-minute timer do?

It gives a visible end to the focus block, which lowers the cost of starting. Stop it whenever you need to.

Can I use it with a paper planner?

Yes. Write the same three items on paper and use the timer here; the point is the limit, not the tool.
